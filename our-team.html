At SGT University's Faculty of Law, we shape leaders who make a meaningful impact in society. Our distinguished legal community tackles today's most pressing challenges – from safeguarding civil liberties and ensuring market fairness to protecting environmental rights and preserving religious freedoms. What sets us apart? We combine academic excellence with practical expertise, offering specialized programs that prepare students for real-world legal challenges. Our strategic location, cutting-edge curriculum, and commitment to ethical practice provide an unmatched educational experience.

We take pride in developing conscientious legal professionals who not only excel in their careers but also serve underserved communities. Join us in our mission to use law as a catalyst for positive social change.

About Our Dean

Distinguished Professors

Mr. Pramod Kumar Dubey

Mr. Pramod Kumar Dubey is a senior advocate at the Supreme Court of India with over 25 years of experience. He is a prominent defense counsel who has handled numerous high-profile cases, including the Tehelka Case, the Coal Scam, and the INX Media Case. His expertise lies primarily in criminal and property law.

Dr. Pavan Duggal

Dr. Pavan Duggal is an internationally recognized expert in cyber law, cybercrime, and cybersecurity. As an advocate at the Supreme Court of India, he is the Founder and Chairman of the International Commission on Cyber Security Law and has written over 200 books on the intersection of law and technology. He has been acknowledged by leading AI platforms, including Google Bard and Gemini, as the world's topmost cyber lawyer.

Mr. Sridhar Potaraju

Mr. Sridhar Potaraju is a senior advocate at the Supreme Court of India with over two decades of practice. He was a key member of the legal team for the plaintiff in the Sri Ramjanmabhoomi case. Beyond his legal practice, he is known for his research into ancient Indian texts and has authored a book titled Maxims from Mahabharata, which compiles Sanskrit shlokas and applies their wisdom to modern life.
