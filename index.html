Faculty of Law

The Faculty of Law (FOL) at SGT University was established in 2014 at SGT University. It has statutory recognition from The Bar Council of India and the Government of Haryana. The Faculty of Law was established to aid the students with education on Legislative Law and provide an academically rich environment. It equips students with a broad perspective enabling them to excel as law professionals & compete effectively on national & international platforms. The Faculty of Law at SGT University is one of the best colleges in Delhi NCR for Law courses. It provides five courses, including UG, PG, Ph.D, Diploma and specialized Certification Courses. The Faculty of Law at SGT University is based on the core values of Dedication, Honesty, Integrity, Respect. It provides the highest quality professional legal education to equip Indian students for transnational practice and to face globalization challengess.

Vision/Mission

Vision

The Faculty of Law, SGT University, aspires to be a premier global institution in legal education, committed to academic excellence and social responsibility, which empowers future legal professionals through transformative learning and a vision for justice that meets the challenges of a changing world.

Mission

The Faculty of Law, SGT University, is dedicated to developing globally competitive and socially committed legal professionals who will shape the future of legal profession by fostering a positive and progressive academic environment rooted in a culture of excellence, well-being, inclusivity, leadership, and holistic development.
