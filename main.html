      The SGT University

      Established in 2013, SGT University is a UGC‑recognized and NAAC A+ accredited institution in Gurgaon, spread over a 70+ acre modern campus. The university focuses on research, innovation, and multidisciplinary education across 20 faculties, offering industry‑aligned programs and global exposure. Its SGT Medical College, Hospital & Research Institute is among India’s leading centers for medical education and healthcare excellence.

      Eminent Personalities & Visionaries

      Celebrating the leaders, innovators, and changemakers who inspire and shape the SGT University community.

      Professor Najeeb Jung is the former Lt. Governor of Delhi, who was the Vice-Chancellor of Jamia Millia Islamia University. Sh. Jung has served in the Asian Development Bank and has been a key driver of energy research and transformation in Asia.Apart from his work in energy, he is a noted translator and poet, his latest work being a masterful translation of Mirza Ghalib.

      Prof. Najeeb Jung
      FORMER LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR

      Mr. Indu Bhushan, a former Indian bureaucrat and economist, served as the first CEO of Ayushman Bharat, revolutionizing healthcare across India. With degrees from top institutions, including Johns Hopkins University, he held key positions at the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank. Mr. Bhushan emphasized the need for more hospital beds in rural areas while managing costs and advocated for digital reforms.

      Sh. Indu Bhushan
      VICE CHAIRMAN, ASIA

      Sandhya is an Independent Director, mentor, and start-up/ scale-up ecosystem partner. As a senior global executive in financial services, she excels in strategic business model innovation, digital transformation, and social impact. She serves as an Independent Director on multiple boards, including Electronica Finance Limited and TTK Prestige.

      Ms. Sandhya Vasudevan
      MEMBER OF THE BOARD, ASIA

      Mr. Rajat M. Nag is an internationally renowned development professional specializing in emerging markets, development issues, strategic visioning, and regional cooperation. He has held several high-ranking positions at the Asian Development Bank(ADB), including the role of Managing Director-General. Mr. Nag's educational background includes degrees from IIT and the London School of Economics.

      Sh. Rajat M. Nag
      MEMBER OF THE BOARD, ASIA

      Dr. Madan Mohan Chaturvedi is presently the Provost of SGT University, Gurugram (Haryana). He taught Biochemistry & Molecular Biology at Banaras Hindu University and University of Delhi over more than 35 years. His research interest are: (i) Chromatin Remodelling and regulation of gene expression, (ii) Inflammation and Cancer, and (iii) Epigenetic regulation of liver regeneration.

      Dr. Madan Mohan Chaturvedi
