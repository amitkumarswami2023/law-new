BSc Neuro‑Physiology Technology Course

The Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Neurophysiology Technology at SGT University, Gurugram, is a unique program that offers students the flexibility to choose between a three-year degree or a four-year honours degree with research. This comprehensive course covers all the essential topics in neurophysiology, with an updated syllabus designed according to international standards. Students gain strong clinical exposure, hands-on training, and research-oriented learning to build successful careers in healthcare and diagnostics.

BSc Neurophysiology Technology Syllabus and Subjects

The syllabus for the BSc in Neurophysiology Technology is meticulously designed to cover essential areas of neurophysiology. Key subjects include:

  • • Neuroanatomy
  • • Neurophysiology
  • • Neuropharmacology
  • • Electroencephalography (EEG)
  • • Nerve Conduction Studies (NCS)
  • • Evoked Potentials (EP)
  • • Polysomnography (PSG)
  • • Neurological Imaging Techniques

The program is structured over six semesters for the three‑year degree and eight semesters for the four‑year honours degree with research. Students can tailor their educational journey to their career goals and academic interests by choosing the appropriate program duration.
