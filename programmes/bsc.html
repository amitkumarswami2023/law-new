BA LLB

The five-year integrated B.A., LL.B. (Hons.) The program stands as our flagship offering, providing comprehensive training across both legal and non legal disciplines. As pioneers of this integrated program in India, Faculty of Law,SGT University designed this curriculum to establish a robust foundation that empowers students to pursue either professional careers or advanced academic endeavors.

Admission to this prestigious program is determined solely through merit. This rigorous selection process ensures we maintain the highest academic standards.

This innovative program structure seamlessly blends legal expertise with broad-based liberal education, creating well-rounded legal professionals equipped to address contemporary challenges.

Teaching Methodology

Innovative Learning Approach

Our law degree program employs diverse teaching methods designed to foster multi-disciplinary inquiry and practical problem-solving skills. The curriculum integrates traditional lectures with interactive discussions, case studies, moot courts, and project work.

A distinctive feature of our methodology is collaborative teaching, where multiple faculty members jointly conduct courses and engage with students simultaneously. This approach particularly enhances the multi-disciplinary analysis of socio-legal issues

Interactive Learning Environment

We maintain a highly interactive classroom environment, supported by comprehensive course materials distributed at the beginning of each semester. These materials include:

  • Detailed course outlines
  • Structured teaching plans
  • Extensive reading lists
  • Study materials comprising case studies
  • Notable judicial decisions
  • Scholarly articles
  • Research reports

Students are expected to prepare using these materials, enabling active participation in classroom discussions

Assessment and Development

Our program emphasizes continuous evaluation and skill development through:

  • Research projects combining literature review and field investigations
  • Activities designed to enhance
  • Research proficiency
  • Analytical capabilities
  • Communication skills

This comprehensive approach ensures students develop both theoretical knowledge and practical expertise necessary for legal practice
